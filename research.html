Valuation laboratory

Adjust growth, terminal margin and exit multiple. All outputs are hypothetical, not forecasts.

Model: forecast revenue = current TTM revenue × (1 + growth)^years; terminal FCF = forecast revenue × selected FCF margin; terminal enterprise value = terminal FCF × exit multiple; terminal equity value = enterprise value + current net cash. It assumes constant shares outstanding, no dividends and no interim cash distributions. These simplifications can materially affect results. Negative terminal FCF produces no meaningful valuation.
